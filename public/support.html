Location Accuracy

Live location works offline using your device GPS and bundled station coordinates. It can show the wrong station when GPS is weak, blocked, or inaccurate. You can always choose stations manually.

Independence

MetroMate Bengaluru is an independent travel utility and is not affiliated with Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited.
